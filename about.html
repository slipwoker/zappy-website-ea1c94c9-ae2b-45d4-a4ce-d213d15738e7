Our Story

Clothing Made for
the Way You Actually Live

ELVI was founded on a simple belief: that well-made clothing should move with you, not against you. We design for layered living — the morning commute, the weekend market, the quiet evening in. Every piece is considered from fabric to finish, rooted in natural materials and made to be worn again and again. Thoughtful consumption isn't a trend for us — it's the only way we know how to work.

Our Materials

Fabric That Feels as Good as It Looks

At ELVI, every garment begins with an honest question: what does this fabric do for the person wearing it? We source breathable, soft-hand textiles from responsible suppliers — chosen for how they feel against skin, how they hold their shape over time, and how gently they sit on the planet.

Breathable Natural Fibres

We work primarily with certified organic cotton, linen, and TENCEL™ lyocell — fibres prized for their natural breathability and moisture-wicking properties. Each weave is selected to keep you comfortable through every season, whether you're moving through a busy day or unwinding at home.

Soft Hand-Feel & Lasting Quality

We reject the trade-off between softness and durability. Our fabrics are rigorously tested for pilling resistance, colour retention, and wash stability so that pieces stay beautiful after years of wear. The softness you feel on day one is the softness you'll feel on day three hundred.

Responsibly Sourced

Every supplier in our textile chain is vetted for fair labour practices and environmental standards. We prioritise mills with GOTS or OEKO-TEX certification, and we keep our supply chain short wherever possible — reducing our footprint and deepening our accountability to the people who make ELVI possible.

From Idea to Wardrobe

Every ELVI piece follows a considered path — from the first sketch to the final fit, each step is shaped by intention, craft, and a commitment to lasting quality.

01

The Idea

Every collection begins with a feeling — a mood, a silhouette, a quiet observation. We sketch freely, letting intuition lead before structure takes over.

02

Fit Refinement

Prototypes are worn, adjusted, and worn again. We refine proportions until movement feels effortless and the garment becomes an extension of the body.

03

Material Selection

Fabrics are chosen for how they feel, how they age, and where they come from. Natural fibres, responsible sourcing, and tactile beauty guide every choice.

04

Wardrobe Cohesion

Each piece is designed to live alongside others — in tone, weight, and purpose. The result is a wardrobe that works as a whole, season after season.

The People Behind ELVI

A Collective of Makers,
Designers & Curators

ELVI is more than a clothing brand — it is a thoughtful gathering of people who believe that what you wear should feel good, be made well, and leave the world a little better. We are designers, storytellers, and responsible makers united by a shared love of style and substance.

Creative Director

Elvi Rosen

Co-Founder & Creative Vision

Elvi started sketching garments on the backs of grocery receipts long before ELVI had a name. Her philosophy is simple: clothing should feel like a second skin — honest, comfortable, and quietly beautiful. She leads every collection with an eye for materials that age gracefully and silhouettes that belong to real life, not just the runway.

Materials & Sourcing

Daniel Kraft

Head of Materials & Sustainability

Daniel travels to small mills and family-run workshops across Europe to find the fabrics that meet our standards — not just in texture and weight, but in the story of how they were made. He is our compass for responsible choices, ensuring every thread in an ELVI piece can be traced back to hands we trust.
