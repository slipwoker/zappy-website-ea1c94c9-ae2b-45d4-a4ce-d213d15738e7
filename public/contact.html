Questions & Answers

Everything You Need to Know

We believe in transparency — from how our garments are made to how we communicate with you.

01

How does ELVI approach sizing?

We design with real bodies in mind. Our sizing runs from XS through 3XL, and each garment page includes detailed measurements alongside fit notes — whether a piece runs relaxed, tailored, or true to size. We encourage you to compare your own measurements rather than rely on a generic size label. If you're between sizes, our team is always happy to guide you toward the right fit.

02

What materials does ELVI use?

Every ELVI piece is crafted from thoughtfully sourced natural and low-impact fabrics — organic cotton, TENCEL™ lyocell, linen blends, and recycled wool. We avoid synthetic materials wherever possible, and all fabric information is listed on each product page. Our goal is clothing that feels as good as it looks, season after season.

03

How should I care for my ELVI garments?

Natural fabrics love gentle care. We recommend cold machine wash or hand wash for most pieces, avoiding harsh detergents and tumble drying. Lay flat or hang to dry to preserve the shape and texture. Each garment includes a care label, and we've also published a full care guide in our journal — because a well-cared-for piece lasts much longer.

04

Do you collaborate with other designers or brands?

Yes — we welcome thoughtful collaborations that align with our values around craft, sustainability, and storytelling. Whether you're an independent designer, a sustainable brand, or a creative studio, we'd love to hear your vision. Reach out via our contact form with a brief introduction and we'll respond within five business days.

05

How quickly can I expect a response?

We aim to respond to all enquiries within 2–3 business days. For order-related questions, our team typically replies within 24 hours. During collection launches or seasonal sales, response times may be slightly longer — we appreciate your patience and promise every message receives a genuine, personal reply.
